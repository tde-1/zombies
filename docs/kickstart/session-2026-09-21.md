## How to work

- **Evidence or it did not happen.** Do not report a thing as working because a call returned without
  error. We have been burned three times by exactly that: injected chat "worked" until we noticed
  nobody had ever seen the text; the 65-second wall was misdiagnosed three times. If you cannot show
  the effect, say "unproven".
- **Retract in writing.** If you reported something that turns out to be wrong, correct it on the
  board explicitly. That log is how the next session avoids re-deriving.
- **Commit and push little and often.** `git commit` with a real message, `git push` to
  https://github.com/tde-1/zombies. Small commits, so a bad one is cheap to revert.
- **Append to `docs/kickstart/board.md`** as you go — the append-only coordination log. Prefix each
  entry with your lane name and the time.
- Tell the manager (the parent session) about anything that needs a decision, rather than guessing at
  something expensive or irreversible.
